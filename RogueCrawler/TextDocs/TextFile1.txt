Damage Investigation

Weapons generate their base damage as follows: 
	BaseDamage = WeaponTypeBaseDamage * MaterialModifier * Log_2(Quality+1)

Creatures then calculate skill, attribute, and stat modifiers:
	SkillLevel = (0.75)*WeaponSkill + (0.25)*WeaponCategorySkill
	SkillInfluence = 1 + Sigmoid(SkillLevel) // Returns a value between 0 and 1
	AttributeInfluence = p_MAJ*0.75 + p_MIN*0.25
	CreatureDamage = BaseDamage * SkillInfluence * AttributeInfluence
	FinalDamage = CreatureDamage * Math.Max(p_Fatigue + Threshold, 1)
	
A creature with maxed skills and no afflictions will double their damage.
