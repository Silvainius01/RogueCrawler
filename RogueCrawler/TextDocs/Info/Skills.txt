Skills are character abilities that are increased by doing relevant tasks, or paying for lessons from various trainers.
All skills are governed by at least one, and commonly two, attributes.

 0-9	Novice
10-29	Apprentice
30-49	Adept
50-69	Expert
70-89	Master
90-100	Grandmaster
