Attributes: 
	Body (Red)
	STR -> Physical strength. Carry load, heavy armor, hand-to-hand damage, and dmg bonus for some weapon types.
	CON -> Consitution. How much damage you can take through conditioning. Can also be interpreted as your resistance to afflictions from various sources.

	Technique (Green)
	AGI -> Agility. Represents whole body control and balance. Think dancing, or being able to weave through a crowd quickly.
	DEX -> Dexterity. Represents fine motor control and flexibility.

	Mind (Blue)
	INT -> Intelligence. Can loosely represent book smarts, and how well your character understands magic on a technical level. 
	WIL -> Willpower. Characters with high willpower can force magical effects into existance, or squeeze more power out of their spells.

	Personality (Yellow)
	CHA -> Charisma. Charisma allows you to convince people, gods, and in some cases, even the fabric of reality itself into granting you favorable terms and outcomes.
	LCK -> Luck. Sometimes, things just go your way.

HP = STR * 2.5 + (CON+1) * 5
FP = (CON+WIL) * 2 + (AGI+1) * 5
MP = (INT+CHA) * 2 + (WIL+1) * 5

Combat Speed = 1 + (DEX * 2 + WIL + (CHA /2)) / 10
	Your actions per round is equal to the floor of your speed value.
	Anything above the floor becomes a % chance to get an additonal action.
	In battle, combat order is determined by DESCENDING speed order. 
	A creature with 2.5 speed goes before one with 2.4 speed, and so on.

Crit Chance = ((LCK * 5) + INT + DEX) / 500

Exp Need Per level: 100 + ((Level - 1) * 200)
	1 -> 100
	2 -> 300
	3 -> 500
	4 -> 700
